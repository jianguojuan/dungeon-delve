  • You can make an account with your email address and a password in the game's Settings, on any phone, and on Android you can sign in with Google Play Games. Your progress then comes back when you change phones or install the game again. Keep your password to yourself: anyone who has it can play your account.

  • You can delete your account at any time in the game's Settings, or by asking us as our deletion page says. Deleting can't be undone. It doesn't refund purchases or cancel a subscription: cancel the Delver Club in your Google Play or App Store settings.

Points, places, leagues and the week's gems are ours to set and may change, and a player we find cheating is kept out of the Arena and may lose what they won there. If you see a name that breaks these rules, or a player you think is cheating, tap them on the leaderboard and choose Report; Block hides them on your device.

If you break these rules, we may remove what you gained by breaking them, take you or your name off the leaderboard, or end your licence.

We may also ban your account for cheating, for example when it would be unfair to other players: a banned account is deleted, and its progress with it, in the cloud and on your device, the next time the game is opened. The chat's rules

The game has a World chat, where players talk with people they don't know. Everyone who uses it agrees to these rules, the game's code of conduct. They also apply to the name you pick, which other players see.

  • Be kind. No bullying, harassment, insults or threats, and no hate against anyone for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or anything else.
  • Keep it for everyone. No sexual talk, nothing violent, cruel or shocking, and no swearing at people. Bad words are starred out, but don't try to get round the filter.
  • No spam or selling. No flooding the chat, ads, begging, links to other sites, servers or games, and no buying, selling or trading accounts, gems, items or anything else.
  • Stay safe. Never share your own or anyone else's real name, address, phone number, school, photos, passwords or account details, and never ask others for theirs. Never agree to meet someone from the game.
  • Be yourself. Don't pretend to be the game's developer, a moderator or another player.
  • Keep the game fair. Don't share cheats, hacks or ways to use bugs: tell us about them instead.
  • Follow the law and Apple's and Google's rules: no scams, gambling or anything illegal.

Reporting. Hold a message to report, block or mute the player who sent it. A report sends us what was said, and we read every report. If someone is in danger, contact your local emergency services first, then write to us at zyrus@zyrusworks.com.

What we may do. We may remove messages and mute or ban a player from the chat or the game, for a while or for good, depending on how serious and how often. Something very serious, such as threats or sexual content involving a child, gets a ban at once, without a warning, and may be passed to the police. A ban turns your account off: while it lasts you can't sign in, chat or keep your progress in the cloud, though you can still play on your device. If you think a ban was a mistake, write to us with your player ID.

Your messages. What you write is yours, and you are responsible for it. By sending it you let us and Unity, who runs the chat for us, show it to other players, keep it for 7 days and check it to keep the chat safe. Other players' messages are theirs, not ours: we don't check them before they appear, but we act on reports.
